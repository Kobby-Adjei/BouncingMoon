# Provider marks

`codex.svg`, `claude.svg`, `gemini.svg`, `copilot.svg`, `muse.svg` are the colour icons from
[LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) v1.104.0 (`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`),
MIT licence, unchanged apart from removing fixed width/height. The one-colour Cursor and
OpenAI marks in `ui.js` come from the same set.

The marks are trademarks of their owners (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, GitHub/Microsoft,
Meta, Anysphere). Relay shows them only to say which provider an agent runs on.
