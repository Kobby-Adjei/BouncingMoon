# Provider marks

`codex.svg`, `claude.svg`, `gemini.svg`, `copilot.svg`, `muse.svg` are the colour icons from
[LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) v1.104.0 (`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`),
MIT licence, unchanged apart from removing fixed width/height. `openai.svg`, `grok.svg` (the set's Grok Bot mark) and
`cursor.svg` are the one-colour icons from the same set, with a style that draws them dark on
light and light on dark; the web app draws the same paths inline.

The marks are trademarks of their owners (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, GitHub/Microsoft,
Meta, Anysphere, xAI). Relay shows them only to say which provider an agent runs on.
